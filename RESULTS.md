## What was measured (conformance-test-backed; all 22 passed)

All measured scenarios are on the one substantially-built detection component,
the **AuthZ-Fuzzer** (plus two platform controls it implements and four
research-validity relations):

- **Cross-tenant / BOLA isolation (S0/S1):** AZ-001, AZ-002, AZ-017, AZ-048,
  PL-027 — the authorization oracle detects cross-tenant reads/writes and denies
  confused-deputy access; **zero isolation escapes**.
- **False-positive discipline (S1/S2):** AZ-004 (public ACL), AZ-006 (404 vs
  leaky-404), AZ-007 (empty-200), AZ-044 (verified/probable/speculative tiers),
  AZ-047 (hardened twin → zero findings) — no false findings.
- **Scope-safety controls (S0):** AZ-029 (host allowlist), AZ-030 (method
  policy), AZ-031 (kill-switch), AZ-033 (rate-limit/budget), AZ-036 (SSRF
  allowlist) — all invariants held.
- **Output/CI integrity (S2):** AZ-043 (SARIF + gate fires only on
  verified/probable), AZ-045 (content-addressed, deterministic evidence).
- **Secret hygiene (S1):** PL-029 (request/response redaction).
- **Research-validity / metamorphic (S1/S2):** RV-005 (field rename), RV-006
  (object-ID permutation), RV-019 (input-order stability), RV-025 (oracle-free
  authz via metamorphic relations) — the authz verdict is invariant under
  semantics-preserving transforms.

## Honest limits

- A green suite means **"survived everything we actually ran"**, never "secure"
  and never "all 326 scenarios executed". Per TSG-1 (Nasr et al. 2510.09023;
  Bhagwatkar et al. 2510.05244), no fixed corpus certifies robustness; the
  adaptive twins and the co-evolving red-team exist precisely because a static
  pass is provisional.
- Every PASS here is a **conformance-test-backed** result: it shows the built
  control has passing tests for the scenario's property. It is **not** a live
  end-to-end scan of a seeded target (that integrated lab is the next step,
  TSG-1 C.3). The claim is exactly that, no more.
- The guide's prose says "286 scenarios" but enumerates **326**; the registry
  keeps all 326 (per-component counts match the guide's G.1 matrix).
